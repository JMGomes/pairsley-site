Support

How can we help?

The answers to common questions are below. For anything else, write to support@pairsley.app. You get an answer from the developer, usually within a day or two.

When does a miss cost a heart?

Only when you could have known better: you turned a card whose partner you had already seen. A miss with two cards you had never seen is free.

How do I get three stars?

Clear the level without losing a heart. Each heart you keep is a star. You can replay any level, and the map keeps your best result.

I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account, open Settings in Pairsley and tap Restore purchases.

Why do I see ads?

The free version shows a short ad after some cleared levels from level 20 on, at most one every three minutes. It never shows an ad after a lost level or in practice. Pairsley Pro removes all ads.

Will I lose my progress on a new phone?

Your progress is part of your phone's iCloud or Google backup. Restore the backup on the new phone and your levels and stars come with it.
