Privacy policy

No account. Your progress stays on your phone.

Effective 27 October 2026. Applies to the Pairsley app for iOS and Android, and to this website.

The short version. Pairsley has no account and no sign-up. Your levels, stars, settings and own decks stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob uses device data to show and measure those ads. Pairsley Pro removes the ads. The Pro purchase goes through the App Store or Google Play, and RevenueCat confirms it with an anonymous ID.

Who is responsible

Pairsley is made by João Manuel Gomes, an independent developer in Zurich, Switzerland. For any question about this policy or your data, write to support@pairsley.app.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the levels you cleared and your best stars.
  • Your settings, such as sound, haptics, card size and the deck you use.
  • Whether you own Pro, so that Pro works offline.
  • The decks you make yourself: their words, and copies of the photos you pick for them. The app asks only for the photos you choose and never uploads them.
  • When you last saw an ad, and when a trial ends, so that the app can keep ads apart and end trials on time.

The developer cannot see this data. Your phone's backup, iCloud or Google, can include it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows a full-screen ad after some cleared levels, from level 20 on. It shows a video ad only when you ask for one: for an extra heart, a 24-hour deck trial or an hour of your own decks. Google AdMob provides these ads. To show, measure and protect them from fraud, AdMob collects data such as your device's advertising ID, your approximate location from your IP address, and how you interact with the ads.

Before the first ad, the app tells you about the ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google then asks for your consent. On iPhone, Apple then asks if the app may track you. If you say no, you still get ads, but they are not personalised.

You can change your choice at any time. Where Google's consent applies, open Settings in Pairsley and tap Ad privacy choices. On iPhone, open the Settings app, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Google explains its use of data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The Pro purchase

Pro is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle your payment, and your payment details never reach the app. RevenueCat confirms and restores the purchase. It receives an anonymous ID that the app makes on your device, the store receipt, and basic technical data such as the app version and the device model. It does not receive your name or your email address. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Usage statistics

Settings has a "Share usage data" switch. It is off unless you turn it on. The current version sends no usage statistics, even when the switch is on. If a later version uses a statistics service, this page will name it first.

This website

pairsley.app has no cookies, no analytics and no ads, and it loads nothing from other sites. GitHub Pages hosts it. GitHub keeps technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect its service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Where data goes

Google, RevenueCat and GitHub can process data in the United States and other countries. They use legal safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, for these transfers. They keep data for as long as their own policies say.

Children

Pairsley is made for adults and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Swiss data protection law and, in the European Union and the UK, the GDPR give you rights over your personal data, such as access and deletion. The developer holds no personal data about you, apart from emails you send to support. For data that Google or RevenueCat hold, see their policies above. For any concern, write to support@pairsley.app. You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Switzerland, this is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

Changes to this policy

When the app changes how it handles data, this page changes too, with a new date at the top.
