Questions

What kind of game is Pairsley?

Pairsley is a memory matching game, also known as Concentration, Pairs or Memory. You turn over two cards at a time and look for the matching pair. It is made for adults who like a calm puzzle at their own pace.

Is Pairsley free?

Yes. You can play every level for free. Pro is an optional one-time purchase that removes the ads and opens every deck.

Is there a subscription?

No. Pro is a single purchase. You pay once and keep it.

Does it need the internet?

No. You can play offline. A connection is only needed for ads, for buying Pro and for restoring it.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes, with Pro, or for an hour after a short ad. Each pair can be a photo or a few words. Your photos stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

I have a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Open Settings in Pairsley and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple or Google account that bought Pro.

What data does Pairsley collect?

There is no account. If you agree, the free version shows ads that may use your phone's advertising ID. The privacy policy has the details.
